iPhone 18 Pro Raises Stakes on Image Provenance

### 2. SEO Description
Apple’s reported Reference Image feature spotlights a growing business need: knowing an image’s origin and edit history.

### 3. LinkedIn Post

A photo’s value increasingly depends on whether its origin can be verified.

- Generative AI makes polished images cheap, weakening visual credibility in listings, claims, reviews, and reports.
- Apple’s reported Reference Image feature could make image-edit history a competitive camera capability.
- A badge alone is insufficient; recipients need a clear, inspectable signal after files are shared, exported, or recompressed.
- The article notes that the feature’s technical mechanics, cross-platform support, and resistance to tampering remain unverified.

The strategic shift is from selling better cameras to building credible chains of image provenance across the places photos actually travel.
