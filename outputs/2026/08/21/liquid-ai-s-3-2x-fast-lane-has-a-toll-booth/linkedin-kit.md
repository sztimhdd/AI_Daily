A 3.18x inference speedup means little if production workloads cannot keep it.

• Liquid AI’s reported DSpark gains target decoding speed—not model knowledge, reasoning, context windows, or retrieval quality.
• The headline figures are vendor-reported and have not been independently reproduced in the available material.
• Business value depends on draft-token acceptance, tail latency, output quality, concurrency, hardware, and cost per accepted answer.
• Teams should shadow-test recent production prompts and retain the existing path wherever quality drifts or memory pressure erases the gain.

The deeper implication: inference optimization should be treated as a workload-specific migration decision, not a foundation-model upgrade.
